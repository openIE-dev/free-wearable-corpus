- **creator**: Leslie A. Geddes et al. (Purdue)
- **disclosure**: Geddes LA, Voelz MH, Babbs CF, Bourland JD, Tacker WA. 'Pulse transit time as an indicator of arterial blood pressure.' Psychophysiology 1981;18(1):71-74.
