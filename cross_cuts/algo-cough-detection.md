## BioIntelliSense BioSticker (2019) — long-wear adhesive chest patch with extensive multi-parameter monitoring (2019-12)

- **corpus**: private
- **form factor**: patch
- **creator**: BioIntelliSense, Inc.
- **disclosure**: BioIntelliSense, Inc.
