## Nike+iPod Sport Kit (2006) — instrumented-shoe accelerometer pod (2006-05-23)

- **disclosure**: Nike, Inc. and Apple Inc. 'Nike+iPod Sport Kit', announced 23 May 2006 — an accelerometer pod that sits in a cavity in a Nike+ running shoe and transmits pace, distance, and calories wirelessly to an iPod nano.
